🔧 HOW IT WORKS (60-Second Explanation)

1. After phase 3, RLM creates a "snapshot" of key information:
   - Architecture decisions (100% preserved)
   - Implementation patterns (summarized 3:1)
   - Technical constraints (100% preserved)
   - Open issues (100% preserved)

2. Instead of passing full conversation history to phase 5 agents, pass snapshot.

3. Result:
   - Phase 5 context: 80k (phase) + 40k (snapshot) = 120k
   - vs. Without RLM: 80k (phase) + 320k (full history) = 400k
   - Savings: 280k tokens on phase 5 alone!

4. Quality preserved because:
   - All critical decisions included
   - Patterns explained enough for context
   - Constraints enforced
   - Issues tracked

📞 QUESTIONS?

Quick Q&A:
  Q: Why 20-30% token savings?
  A: Compression ratio 25%, applies to context size (not all tokens)

  Q: When does RLM start?
  A: After phase 3 (need 3+ phases of history to compress)

  Q: Will outputs be worse?
  A: No! Preserved all critical decisions, only summarized patterns

  Q: Can I disable RLM?
  A: Yes! Toggle in settings, or set rlmEnabled = false

  Q: What about short projects (1-2 phases)?
  A: RLM won't trigger (< 3 phases). No benefit anyway.

  Q: Can I customize compression?
  A: Yes! Adjust targetTokenBudget parameter (default 2000)
